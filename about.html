About WISEINN

Every Space Holds Potential

WISEINN is a multi-disciplinary company dedicated to transforming homes, outdoor spaces and commercial properties into functional, stylish and sustainable environments — through one brand and three distinct divisions.

Who We Are

One Brand. A Foundation in Landscaping and Construction.

With a strong foundation in landscaping and construction, WISEINN offers a comprehensive range of services including painting, plumbing, paving, tiling, gardening, refurbishment, revamping, swimming pool installation, pergola construction, general maintenance and full home improvement solutions. Our team is made up of skilled professionals who share a passion for excellence, attention to detail and customer satisfaction.

Whether it's a simple garden refresh or a complete property transformation, we work closely with our clients to bring their vision to life while maintaining the highest standards of safety, quality and craftsmanship. Our commitment to integrity, reliability and superior service has earned us a reputation as a trusted partner for homeowners, businesses and property developers seeking long-lasting results and exceptional value.

Today, that same standard runs through all three WISEINN divisions — Wiseinn Landscaping & Outdoor Design, SEEFAKO Projects and Design and WISEINN Grilladin.

Where We're Going

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

Setting New Standards in Property Transformation

Our vision is to become a leading, nationally recognised landscaping and projects company known for excellence, creativity and reliability. We strive to build lasting relationships, uplift communities through quality craftsmanship, and set new standards in property transformation across South Africa.

Our Mission

Elevating Every Space We Touch

Our mission is to elevate every space we touch by blending craftsmanship, creativity and care. We are dedicated to delivering sophisticated landscaping and home improvement solutions that enhance comfort, beauty and long-term value. Through exceptional service, honest partnership and unwavering attention to detail, we aim to create environments that our clients are proud to live in, work in, and showcase.
